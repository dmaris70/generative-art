Trilogie de la Mort is Éliane Radigue's magnum opus: three hour-long electronic meditations on death and the states of consciousness surrounding it, composed between 1985 and 1993 on the ARP 2500 modular synthesizer and released as a triple CD by Phill Niblock's Experimental Intermedia label (XI 119) in 1998. [[1](https://elianeradigue.bandcamp.com/album/trilogie-de-la-mort), [2](https://www.discogs.com/release/415731-Eliane-Radigue-Trilogie-De-La-Mort)]

### 📿 The Bardo

Radigue, a practising Tibetan Buddhist, grounded the trilogy in the *Bardo Thödol* — the so-called Tibetan Book of the Dead. The first part, Kyema, refers directly to the root text's six intermediate states of consciousness (bardos) traversed between life, death and rebirth; the whole cycle is structured as that passage. [[1](https://elianeradigue.bandcamp.com/album/trilogie-de-la-mort)]

### 🎛 One synthesizer, infinite patience

Every part was realized on Radigue's ARP 2500 — the instrument she worked with almost exclusively for three decades — recorded to magnetic tape at her Paris studio (Koumé was completed at Studio CIRM in Nice, 1993). Her method was the opposite of the synthesizer demo: near-static fields of beating partials whose transformations are so gradual they are perceived only after they have happened. Nothing "occurs"; everything changes. [[1](https://elianeradigue.bandcamp.com/album/trilogie-de-la-mort), [2](https://www.soundohm.com/product/trilogie-de-la-mort-3cd)]

### 📅 Three parts, eight years

* **Kyema** (1988, 61'07) — premiered 17 September 1988 at New Langton Arts, San Francisco.
* **Kailasha** (1991, 56'10) — an imagined circuit of the sacred Mount Kailash; premiered 16 March 1991 at the Experimental Intermedia Foundation, New York.
* **Koumé** (begun 1991, finished 1993) — the trilogy's darkest panel, completed after the death of her son Yves Arman, which marked the project profoundly. [[1](https://elianeradigue.bandcamp.com/album/trilogie-de-la-mort), [2](https://www.discogs.com/release/415731-Eliane-Radigue-Trilogie-De-La-Mort)]

### 🧬 Legacy

Long an insiders' secret, the Trilogie is now widely regarded as Radigue's masterpiece and a summit of electronic drone music; it has been presented complete in marathon listening sessions (Blank Forms among others has staged it) and stands behind the wave of recognition that reached her late in life, when she turned from the ARP to acoustic instruments in the Occam series. [[1](https://www.blankforms.org/events/eliane-radigue-trilogie-de-la-mort), [2](https://elianeradigue.bandcamp.com/album/trilogie-de-la-mort)]
