Live at Issue Project Room documents the first-ever US performance of Catherine Christer Hennix's expanded just-intonation ensemble, the Chora(s)san Time-Court Mirage — an eighty-minute drone work recorded at ISSUE Project Room, Brooklyn, on 23 April 2014 (during the Ultima Festival New York) and released on CD by Important Records (IMPREC 434) in 2016. Note on the catalogue: sources agree the concert was 2014 and the release 2016; Important's own product page is vague on dating, so the 2016 release year follows the distributor listing and Discogs. [[1](https://issueprojectroom.org/event/catherine-christer-hennix-chorassan-time-court-mirage), [2](https://www.forcedexposure.com/Catalog/hennix-catherine-christer-live-at-issue-project-room-cd/IMPREC.434CD.html), [3](https://www.discogs.com/master/2000578-Chorassan-Time-Court-Mirage-Live-At-Issue-Project-Room)]

### 🎼 The Work

The single piece is titled *Blues Alif Lam Mim in the Mode of Rag Infinity/Rag Cosmosis (1434 A.H.)* — a title splicing Qur'anic letters, Hindustani raga nomenclature and the Islamic calendar. Hennix's program aimed to "reveal the blues' origins in the eastern musical traditions of raga and maqam musics," folding Delta blues intonation, dhrupad and Sufi song into one sustained modal continuum. [[1](https://issueprojectroom.org/event/catherine-christer-hennix-chorassan-time-court-mirage)]

### 🤝 The Ensemble

The Chora(s)san Time-Court Mirage here reached its largest configuration:

* **Voices:** Imam Ahmet Muhsin Tüzer (a Turkish imam and Sufi singer), Amir ElSaffar, Amirtha Kidambi, with Hennix herself
* **Brass:** Paul Schwingenschlögl, Elena Kakaliagou — with Hilary Jeffery (trombone) and Robin Hayward (microtonal tuba) named in the concert listing
* **Electronics:** Marcus Pal and Stefan Tiedje, sustaining the just-intonation drone environment [[1](https://issueprojectroom.org/event/catherine-christer-hennix-chorassan-time-court-mirage), [2](https://www.forcedexposure.com/Catalog/hennix-catherine-christer-live-at-issue-project-room-cd/IMPREC.434CD.html)]

### 🔊 Just Intonation and the Time-Court

Hennix — Swedish mathematician, logician and composer, collaborator of La Monte Young and student of Pandit Pran Nath — tunes the entire environment in just intonation: voices and instruments lock onto whole-number frequency ratios over an electronic drone, producing stationary, shimmering combination tones. Forced Exposure notes the piece is her first four-channel computer-assisted composition since 1969, connecting it back to her pioneering *Electric Harpsichord* era. [[1](https://www.forcedexposure.com/Catalog/hennix-catherine-christer-live-at-issue-project-room-cd/IMPREC.434CD.html)]

### 🧬 Significance

Issued in a letterpressed sleeve as part of Important's Hennix series, the disc is a rare full-scale document of a composer whose work was mostly unrecorded for decades, and captures the late flowering — mixing sacred vocal traditions into mathematical drone — that defined her final decade before her death in 2023. [[1](https://importantrecords.com/products/catherine-christer-hennix-live-at-issue-project-room)]
