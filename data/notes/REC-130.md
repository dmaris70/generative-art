L'Expérience acoustique is François Bayle's roughly two-hour acousmatic cycle, realized between 1966 and 1972 at the Groupe de Recherches Musicales (GRM) in Paris — the studio founded around Pierre Schaeffer's musique concrète, which Bayle was appointed to lead in 1966. It is one of the largest single works of the acousmatic repertoire: not a suite of pieces but a systematic, multi-part investigation of what listening itself is. [[1](https://boomkat.com/products/l-experience-acoustique), [2](https://www.soundohm.com/product/l-experience-acoustique-3)]

### 🗂 A cycle in five parts

The work unfolds as five major sections, each dated within the cycle's six-year gestation:

* **L'Aventure du cri** (1969)
* **Le Langage des fleurs** (1971)
* **La Preuve par le sens** (1971)
* **L'Épreuve par le son** (1971)
* **La Philosophie du non** (1972) — its title borrowed from Gaston Bachelard's book on the philosophy of scientific negation. [[1](https://boomkat.com/products/l-experience-acoustique), [2](https://www.discogs.com/release/4897441-Fran%C3%A7ois-Bayle-LExp%C3%A9rience-Acoustique)]

### 🎧 Music about listening

Bayle's material is the recorded world — voices, instruments, environments, electronics — transformed and juxtaposed with unusual textural care into what one description calls immersive, intricate sound ecologies. The "experiment" of the title is meant seriously: the cycle exemplifies GRM's "musical research" ethos, treating composition as a laboratory for the acousmatic situation, where sound is heard from loudspeakers with no visible cause and the ear must construct the world itself. [[1](https://boomkat.com/products/l-experience-acoustique), [2](https://www.soundohm.com/product/l-experience-acoustique-3)]

### ⚡ The GRM studio

As head of GRM from 1966, Bayle had access to the most advanced electroacoustic facilities in France, and later invented the Acousmonium loudspeaker orchestra for projecting such works in concert. L'Expérience acoustique sits at the hinge between Schaeffer's founding generation and the spatialized acousmatic concert culture Bayle built. [[1](https://www.soundohm.com/product/l-experience-acoustique-3), [2](https://electrodoc.musiques-recherches.be/en/c/121-francois-bayle)]

### 🧬 Editions

The cycle circulated on INA-GRM LPs and CDs and was reissued in the Recollection GRM series (REGRM 010, Editions Mego), which returned much of the classic GRM catalogue to print; it is also available digitally through Bayle's Bandcamp. Detailed premiere and version history is thinly documented in English — the dates above follow the reissue's own notes.
