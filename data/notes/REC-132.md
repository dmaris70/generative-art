Requiem (1973) by Michel Chion is a full requiem mass composed on tape — a noisy, surreal deconstruction and recreation of the Latin funeral liturgy, realized in the Paris studios of the INA-GRM on their commission, and widely regarded as one of the defining works of the musique concrète canon. On the date: the piece was completed and premiered in 1973 (19 March 1973, Théâtre Récamier, Paris); the frequently seen "1978" is the year it won the Grand Prix du Disque of the Académie du Disque Français, and the INA-GRM LP appeared in that period — so both dates circulate, but 1973 is the composition date. [[1](https://motuscompagniemusicale.bandcamp.com/album/requiem-laudes), [2](https://dangerousminds.net/comments/michel_chions_1973_musique_concrete_masterpiece_requiem/), [3](https://www.discogs.com/release/197727-Michel-Chion-Requiem)]

### ⛪ A Mass on Magnetic Tape

The roughly 37-minute work sets the funeral mass in two parts and ten movements, keeping the liturgical order — Introit through Libera me — and adding an Epistle, Gospel and Our Father, sung and spoken in Latin, Greek and occasionally French. [[1](https://motuscompagniemusicale.bandcamp.com/album/requiem-laudes)]

* **Materials:** voices (the ensemble Le Madrigal de Paris plus soloists), acoustic and instrumental sources including crumhorn, celesta and organ, and electronic sound — all composed directly onto tape. [[1](https://motuscompagniemusicale.bandcamp.com/album/requiem-laudes)]
* **Technique:** classic concrète manipulation in the GRM lineage of Schaeffer (Chion was a member of the Groupe de Recherches Musicales and later its historian), but pushed toward the theatrical and grotesque — whispers, shrieks, abrupt cuts and silences.

### 🪞 Symmetry and the Torn Tape

The work is built on a system of echoes and correspondences arranged symmetrically around a central axis at the sixth movement, the Gospel, where a symbolic rupture of the tape itself occurs — in Chion's words opening "a gap of eternity in the flow of time." The medium's own fragility becomes the theological argument. [[1](https://motuscompagniemusicale.bandcamp.com/album/requiem-laudes)]

### 🕯️ A Personal Testimony

Chion insisted the Requiem was a personal testimony rather than a religious manifesto — an exploration of fundamental uncertainty about life, death and faith onto which listeners project their own experience. [[1](https://motuscompagniemusicale.bandcamp.com/album/requiem-laudes)]

### 🧬 Afterlife

Long a cult object among collectors of the INA-GRM catalogue, the Requiem has been reissued on CD and digitally, and was given a 50th-anniversary acousmatic performance in 2023, diffused over loudspeaker orchestra by the Motus company, with whom Chion has long worked. Chion himself became better known as a theorist of film sound (*Audio-Vision*), which makes this early masterpiece read in retrospect like his theories staged as ritual.
