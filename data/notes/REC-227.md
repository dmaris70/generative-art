unitxt (2008) is the fifth studio album by Carsten Nicolai under his Alva Noto alias, released 22 July 2008 on raster-noton (r-n095), the Chemnitz label he co-founded with Olaf Bender and Frank Bretschneider. The title compresses the project's programme — *unit* (element, unit of measurement) plus *txt* (data, language) — and the album is the purest statement of Nicolai's grid aesthetics: rhythm generated from mathematics and raw data rather than from musical gesture. [[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitxt), [2](https://boomkat.com/products/unitxt-e566c3e4-2bba-4a91-bc37-051448b06177)]

### ⚡ Data as sound

The album's most quoted conceit is its closing run of micro-tracks in which files and applications are converted directly into audio — Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Fontlab and other software rendered as raw streams of clicks and noise-bursts, each track named for its source program [[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitxt), [2](https://dezji.wordpress.com/2008/07/28/album-alva-noto-unitxt-raster-noton-r-n095-2008/)]. Nothing is "composed" in these fragments: the sound *is* the data structure, sonification as ready-made. After the opening sequence of full compositions, sixteen-plus such miniatures fragment the record's second half.

### 🥁 The grid

The core tracks apply mathematical pattern-generation to club tempos — brutally quantized 4/4 around 120 bpm, built from sine tones, impulse clicks and bandpassed noise with laboratory precision. Reviewers noted the "curious dichotomy" of clinical process music aimed squarely at the body [[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitxt)]. The material was developed live during raster-noton's 2006–07 Japan tours and edited in Berlin in 2008, and unitxt launched a trilogy continued by *univrs* (2011) [[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitxt)].

### 🗣️ Anne-James Chaton

French sound-poet Anne-James Chaton voices several tracks, reciting inventories of data in deadpan monotone — on "Uni Acronym" a litany of acronyms — so that spoken language enters the record already formatted, like everything else on it, as units of information [[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitxt), [2](https://boomkat.com/products/unitxt-e566c3e4-2bba-4a91-bc37-051448b06177)].

### 🧬 Significance

unitxt distilled a decade of raster-noton's "microscopic sound" — the label's fusion of white-cube minimalism, information theory and techno — into its most legible form, and it remains a reference point for data sonification as aesthetics rather than utility. Nicolai's parallel career as a visual artist (as Carsten Nicolai) is audible throughout: the album functions like one of his installations, a system exhibited in operation, its beauty located in the exactness of the conversion itself [[1](https://www.discogs.com/master/9524-Alva-Noto-Unitxt), [2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitxt)].
