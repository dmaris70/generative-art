Contraposition (2018) is a four-track 12" EP by the Rotherham producer Rian Treanor, and the record that relaunched Arcola — Warp's experimental club sub-label — after fourteen years of dormancy. A note on the catalogue entry: this release is sometimes conflated with Treanor's debut *album*, but the two are distinct — Contraposition came out on Arcola in 2018, and the full-length ATAXIA followed on Planet Mu on 15 March 2019. [[1](https://www.factmag.com/2018/01/18/warp-arcola-relaunch-rian-treanor-ep/), [2](https://planet.mu/releases/ataxia/)]

### 🔁 Pattern Permutation

Treanor — son of the computer musician Mark Fell, and trained in art school as much as in club culture — builds tracks by algorithmic manipulation of small rhythmic and melodic cells: generating series of irregular events and restructuring them, or taking a steady pattern and systematically destabilizing it. The listener can identify and remember the underlying pattern, yet it is never played straight; the music flickers between grid and glitch. Contraposition shows the darker, more techno-facing side of this method than his earlier dancehall- and footwork-inflected records for The Death of Rave. [[1](https://planet.mu/releases/ataxia/), [2](https://boomkat.com/products/contraposition-ep), [3](https://shapeplatform.eu/artist/rian-treanor/)]

### ⚡ The Arcola Relaunch

Warp announced the EP on 18 January 2018, reviving Arcola as a limited-12" imprint dedicated to "the spaces where contemporary electronic music meets club music." The vinyl was limited to 500 copies, with a debossed sleeve by The Designers Republic — the design studio synonymous with Warp's classic 1990s look, a deliberate signal of lineage. [[1](https://www.factmag.com/2018/01/18/warp-arcola-relaunch-rian-treanor-ep/), [2](https://www.thevinylfactory.com/news/warp-revives-arcola-sub-label-rian-treanor-ep), [3](https://www.discogs.com/Rian-Treanor-Contraposition/release/11434806)]

### 🥁 Sound

Across its four entries the EP mixes subzero digital tones with sharply swung, permutated drum programming — hyper-chromatic synth stabs and chopped vocal samples arranged in sequences that feel simultaneously danceable and wrong-footed, club music with the logic of a combinatorics exercise. [[1](https://boomkat.com/products/contraposition-ep), [2](https://bleep.com/release/94241-rian-treanor-contraposition-ep)]

### 🧬 Toward ATAXIA

The EP set up Treanor's 2019 Planet Mu debut ATAXIA, whose title (the loss of full control of bodily movements) makes the project explicit: music "intended to make people's bodies move in unpredictable ways." That album — rewiring UK garage and pointillist footwork through the same permutation systems — confirmed him as one of the most distinctive algorithmic voices in British club music. [[1](https://planet.mu/releases/ataxia/), [2](https://riantreanor.bandcamp.com/album/ataxia)]
