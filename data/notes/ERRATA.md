# Catalogue queries — DMCF v1 buy list

Discrepancies between the v1 CSV and the documented record, surfaced during the
research pass of 2026-07-28. Each is argued with sources inside the work's own note;
this is the index. "Query" means the note flags it — verify before correcting the CSV.

## Attribution / identity

| id | catalogue says | research found |
|---|---|---|
| REC-139 | *Voi(rex)*, Annette Vande Gorne, 1993 | **Voi(rex) is by Philippe Leroux** (IRCAM 2002, premiered 2003). The Vande Gorne work almost certainly intended is her *Vox Alia* cycle (begun 1992). |
| REC-190 | *Relations & Assimilations*, Lily Greenham, 1975 | **Title not found anywhere in Greenham's documented catalogue** (checked the full *Lingual Music* tracklist, Bandcamp, estate site). Her 1973–75 works are *Relativity*, *Traffic*, *7 Consonants in Space*. |
| REC-044 | *EARS*, holder RVNG Intl | **EARS is on Western Vinyl.** Smith's 2016 RVNG release was *Sunergy* (with Suzanne Ciani). |
| REC-088 | *Composition 6E*, holder Arista, 1974 | 6E was recorded **27 March 1968, Chicago, for Delmark** (*3 Compositions of New Jazz*), not in the Arista period. |
| REC-133 | *Points*, holder New World Records | The 2020 *Here* LP carrying Points is on **Arc Light Editions**; New World's connection is via the 1977 CRI *Women in Electronic Music* anthology. |
| REC-067 | *Music from the Morning of the World: Ketjak*, 1969 | The **full ketjak is on *Golden Rain* (1969)**; *Morning of the World* (1967) carries only an excerpt. Title/date pairing is mixed. |
| REC-183 | holder Sub Rosa | Sub Rosa release **unconfirmed**; recordings verified on Made to Measure (1993) and *Recordings 1960–81* (1995). |

## Worth knowing (not errors)

- REC-179 *Ursonate*: only the 1932 Scherzo is documented as Kurt Schwitters' own voice; the 1993 WERGO "complete" recording was identified by 2006 voice analysis as his son Ernst's performance.
- REC-314 *Rainforest II*: per the Driscoll/Rogalsky paper, II is the vocal-input version with no documented standalone performances (likely realized inside Cunningham Events); the New World CD mislabels the 1972 *Rainforest III* as II.
- REC-118: *Contraposition* is the 2018 Arcola EP; *ATAXIA* is the separate Planet Mu debut (2019). CSV's pairing of title/label is internally consistent but worth knowing.
- REC-069: Arom analyzed Aka polyphony as variational counterpoint and resisted the "hocket" label — contested term.
