Presque rien No. 1 « Le lever du jour au bord de la mer » (1967–70) by Luc Ferrari is one of the most quietly radical pieces in the electroacoustic canon: twenty-odd minutes of daybreak in a Yugoslav fishing village, presented almost untouched as a composition. Assembled from field recordings Ferrari made in July 1967 in Vela Luka, on the Dalmatian island of Korčula, it was completed at Deutsche Grammophon's Hannover studios from 28 January to 3 February 1970 and premiered in concert on 25 October 1970 at Nutida Musik in Stockholm. [[1](https://brahms.ircam.fr/works/work/36417/), [2](https://www.soundohm.com/product/presque-rien-n-1-le-lever-1)]

### 🌍 The Dawn Itself

Ferrari set his microphone at the window before dawn and let the village compose:

* a donkey braying, the first motor of a fishing boat
* footsteps on cobbles, hushed chatter of villagers, cicadas
* the rattle of a truck engine as the day's work begins [[1](https://www.soundohm.com/product/presque-rien-n-1-le-lever-1)]

The dramaturgy is the day's own: sounds enter as the light rises, and the piece ends when the boats have gone out.

### 🎧 "Almost Nothing"

The title — "almost nothing" — is the aesthetic. Ferrari, a founding figure of the Groupe de Recherches Musicales, here dispensed with electronic transformation altogether, condensing many hours of location tape into a continuous, apparently unmanipulated morning; he called the result "a sort of anti-music." [[1](https://sunra.substack.com/p/luc-ferrari)] The composition hides in the editing: time is compressed, events selected and layered, but the seams are deliberately inaudible. This "anecdotal music" broke with his GRM colleagues' doctrine of the abstract sound object by insisting that sounds keep their stories.

### 📻 Release

The piece appeared on LP in Deutsche Grammophon's avant-garde series, coupled with Ferrari's *Société II* — a major-label imprimatur for a piece consisting, on its face, of nothing but a village waking up. [[1](https://www.discogs.com/release/267453-Luc-Ferrari-Presque-Rien-No1-Soci%C3%A9t%C3%A9-II), [2](https://store.deutschegrammophon.com/en-en/products/luc-ferrari-luc-ferrari-presque-rien-nr-1-societe-2-111809)]

### 🧬 Legacy

Presque rien No. 1 pre-empted soundscape composition, field-recording culture and much of what is now called phonography; three further *Presque rien* pieces followed over thirty years. The score/edition is now kept by Maison ONA, and the work is a fixture of every history of listening as composition.
