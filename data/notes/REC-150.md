Energy Field (Touch, TO:73, April 2010) is Norwegian artist Jana Winderen's breakthrough album: three long composed pieces built from hydrophone recordings made in the Barents Sea north of Norway and Russia, in Greenland, and in her native Norway — deep in glacier crevasses, in fjords, and in the open ocean [[1](https://touch33.net/catalogue/to73-jana-winderen-energy-field.html), [2](https://www.janawinderen.com/releases/energy-field), [3](https://www.discogs.com/release/2175193-Jana-Winderen-Energy-Field)]. It made the case that the underwater Arctic, far from silent, is one of the densest acoustic environments on the planet.

### 🎧 The Equipment

Winderen's method is documentary in its rigour. Energy Field was captured with four DPA 8011 hydrophones, DPA 4060 omnidirectional microphones, a Telinga parabolic reflector and a Sound Devices 744T hard-disk recorder [[1](https://touch33.net/catalogue/to73-jana-winderen-energy-field.html), [2](https://janawinderen.bandcamp.com/album/energy-field)]. Hydrophones lowered tens of metres down open water and into ice fissures pick up what no ear at the surface can reach.

### 🔊 What Is Heard

The recordings catch crustaceans clicking, and cod, haddock, herring and pollock — sounds the fish make while hunting, calling for mates and orienting themselves — alongside the strain and fracture of glacial ice and the deep weather of moving water [[1](https://touch33.net/catalogue/to73-jana-winderen-energy-field.html), [2](https://www.janawinderen.com/releases/energy-field)]. None of it is synthesised; the alien character of the album is entirely a property of the sources.

### 🔁 Composition, Not Documentation

Winderen edits and layers her material into what Touch calls "a powerful descriptive soundscape" [[1](https://touch33.net/catalogue/to73-jana-winderen-energy-field.html)]: the pieces are composed journeys, arranged for dramatic arc rather than scientific neutrality — field recording treated as an instrumental palette, in the lineage of Touch labelmates like Chris Watson [[2](https://www.allmusic.com/album/energy-field-mw0001970250)].

### 🧬 Listening as Ecology

The album's lasting significance is ethical as much as sonic. By making audible the biological density of cold-water ecosystems — precisely the ecosystems most exposed to warming and industrial noise — Energy Field turned deep listening into an environmental argument, and established Winderen as a central figure in the field now called acoustic ecology. Her subsequent installations and releases have extended the same practice; this record remains its clearest single statement [[1](https://www.janawinderen.com/releases/energy-field)].
