A flag before anything else: this catalogue entry appears to carry a misattributed title. **Voi(rex) is not by Annette Vande Gorne** — it is a 2002 work by the French composer Philippe Leroux for voice, six instruments and electronics, premiered at IRCAM in Paris on 10 January 2003 by Donatienne Michel-Dansac and Ensemble L'Itinéraire. [[1](https://brahms.ircam.fr/works/work/14267/)] The Vande Gorne work the entry almost certainly intends is her acousmatic voice cycle **Vox Alia**, whose earliest music dates from the early 1990s — consistent with the catalogue's 1993 date. The notes below describe Vox Alia, with the caveat that the exact 1993 attribution could not be pinned to a single dated movement in available sources.

### 🎤 Vox Alia

Vox Alia is a five-part cycle composed across three decades (1992–2024), with the human voice as its sole subject: Affetti (I), Cathédrales (II), Vox intima (III, on a text by Werner Lambersy), Vox populi (IV) and Vox naturæ (V). The opening work, Vox Alia I — Affetti, was realized between 1992 and 2000 in five movements (Giocoso, Amoroso, Innocentemente, Furioso, Parola volante), using sound material processed on Ina-GRM's SYTER and MARS systems. The full cycle was finally collected on disc by the Montréal label empreintes DIGITALes on 9 March 2025. [[1](https://empreintesdigitales.bandcamp.com/album/vox-alia), [2](http://electrocd.com/en/cat/imed_25196)]

### 🌍 The Belgian Acousmatic School

Vande Gorne (b. 1946) is the central figure of Belgian acousmatics: founder of Musiques & Recherches and its Métamorphoses d'Orphée studio in Ohain, organizer of the L'Espace du son festival, and a tireless theorist of spatialization — music composed for, and performed on, orchestras of loudspeakers. A choirmaster since 1965, she treats the studio as a way of extending what a voice can do while keeping its melodic and rhythmic identity intact. [[1](https://empreintesdigitales.bandcamp.com/album/vox-alia), [2](https://panm360.com/en/interviews-panm360/semaine-du-neuf-ecouter-les-yeux-fermes-la-musique-acoustique-selon-annette-vande-gorne/)]

### 🔊 What the Cycle Does

Each part probes a different register of vocality: pure voice and vocal "affects," sacred song and resonant architecture, the intimate interior voice, the collective voice of crowds, and voice as a sound of nature. Spoken text is dispersed and reintegrated into the musical flow; screams, in-breaths and dialogue are dramatized by context; spatial writing — Vande Gorne's specialty — carries much of the expression. [[1](https://empreintesdigitales.bandcamp.com/album/vox-alia)]

### 🧬 Why It Matters Here

As a system-piece, Vox Alia is a taxonomy: one parameter (the voice) systematically traversed through its possible states over thirty years. If the catalogue's 1993 date and Voi(rex) title are ever corrected, this entry should point at Vox Alia I — Affetti; if a Leroux work was genuinely intended, the note above gives its true coordinates.
