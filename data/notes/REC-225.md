datamatics (2006–) is Ryoji Ikeda's long-running art project testing whether pure data — the invisible substrate of the digital world — can be perceived aesthetically. Conceived as "a series of experiments in various forms — audiovisual concerts, installations, publications and CD releases," it premiered as an audiovisual concert in March 2006, co-commissioned by AV Festival 06, ZeroOne San Jose and ISEA 2006. [[1](https://www.ryojiikeda.com/project/datamatics/), [2](https://forma.org.uk/projects/datamatics)]

### 💾 Data as Material

Ikeda's sources are deliberately unglamorous: hard-drive errors, studies of software code, scientific data sets. On screen these are rendered as pared-down black-and-white computer graphics with occasional striking colour accents, progressing from 2D pattern sequences through dramatic rotating 3D views of the universe to four-dimensional mathematical processing. The sound is equally reduced — sine tones, clicks and white noise at the thresholds of perception, layered into what the project text calls "immense and apparently boundless acoustic spaces." [[1](https://www.ryojiikeda.com/project/datamatics/), [2](https://special.ycam.jp/datamatics/dl/datamatics_release_en.pdf)]

### ⚡ Versions and Collaborators

* **prototype-ver.1.0** (2006–08): the first concert version.
* **ver.2.0** (2008): the full-length concert with a commissioned second part, co-produced by Les Spectacles vivants – Centre Pompidou and the Yamaguchi Center for Arts and Media (YCAM). [[1](https://www.ryojiikeda.com/project/datamatics/)]
* Programming by Shohei Matsukawa, Daisuke Tsunoda, Tomonaga Tokuyama and Norimichi Hirakawa — Ikeda works with a team of coders the way a director works with a crew. [[1](https://www.ryojiikeda.com/project/datamatics/)]
* On record, the project connects to Ikeda's data-driven albums on Raster-Noton, beginning with *dataplex* (2005). [[1](https://www.ryojiikeda.com/project/datamatics/)]

### 🎧 The Concert Experience

A datamatics performance immerses the audience in synchronised sound and image at extreme precision and volume: minimalist to the point of asceticism, yet physically overwhelming — data sublime rather than data visualisation. Institutions from the Pompidou to LABoral have presented it as both concert and installation exhibition. [[1](https://laboralcentrodearte.org/en/exhibitions/ryoji-ikeda-datamatics-2/), [2](https://forma.org.uk/projects/datamatics)]

### 🧬 Significance

datamatics fixed the vocabulary of twenty-first-century data aesthetics: the black-and-white barcode fields, the sub-bass and ultrasonics, the mathematical sublime. Its successors in Ikeda's own catalogue (*test pattern*, *superposition*, *micro | macro*) and countless imitators in media art descend directly from it, making it a canonical statement that data is not a representation of the world but a material in its own right. [[1](https://www.ryojiikeda.com/project/datamatics/)]
