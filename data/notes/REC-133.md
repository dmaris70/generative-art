Points (1973–74) is a tape piece by the American composer Ruth Anderson built from nothing but sine tones — pure, harmonic-free electronic sound deployed not as laboratory material but as balm. Composed in the electronic music studio Anderson founded at Hunter College in New York, it is a five-and-a-half-minute meditation that she explicitly intended as "a healing piece". [[1](https://www.thewire.co.uk/in-writing/essays/ruth-anderson-uncaged-music)]

### 🔊 Sine tones, liberated

In the elektronische Musik tradition the sine wave stood for numerical purity and rigor. Anderson turned that on its head after noticing that her electronic-music students felt "calm and energised" when listening to sine tones in class. The Wire's essay on her describes Points as "seven tidal surges against six eddying whorls of sine waves", with a new tone entering roughly every five seconds — gentle, lapping, almost amniotic when played quietly. [[1](https://www.thewire.co.uk/in-writing/essays/ruth-anderson-uncaged-music)]

### 🤝 A studio pioneer at Hunter College

Anderson (1928–2019) founded, designed and ran the Hunter College Electronic Music Studio from 1968 — making her one of the very few women anywhere then directing an electronic music studio. At Hunter she met the composer Annea Lockwood; the two became lifelong partners and eventually spouses, together researching the healing properties of sound and co-teaching "Living Women, Living Music", a pioneering course on women's contributions to music. [[1](https://www.thewire.co.uk/in-writing/essays/ruth-anderson-uncaged-music), [2](https://4columns.org/dayal-geeta/ruth-anderson-and-annea-lockwood)]

### 🎧 Listening as the work

Points belongs to Anderson's turn toward meditative, psychoacoustic composition alongside its sibling piece SUM, begun the same year. The music asks for a particular mode of attention — low volume, long focus — and rewards it with slow beating patterns and spatial illusions as the sine tones interfere. It anticipates by decades the current interest in sound-as-care and deep listening. [[1](https://www.thewire.co.uk/in-writing/essays/ruth-anderson-uncaged-music), [2](https://newmusicusa.org/nmbx/hearing-a-person-remembering-ruth-anderson-1928-2019/)]

### 🧬 Release history — a note on labels

Points first circulated on the landmark 1977 anthology *New Music for Electronic and Recorded Media: Women in Electronic Music*, and the track is today distributed by New World Records. [[1](https://newworldrecords.bandcamp.com/track/points), [2](https://www.thewire.co.uk/in-writing/essays/ruth-anderson-uncaged-music)] The 2020 retrospective LP *Here* — whose test pressing Anderson approved just before her death in 2019, collecting SUM, Points and *I come out of your sleep* — was released by **Arc Light Editions**, not New World/CRI as sometimes catalogued; the catalogue's attribution should be read with that correction. [[1](https://ruthanderson.bandcamp.com/album/here-arc-light-editions), [2](https://lorettagoldberg.com/a-different-way-of-being-human-mp3-vinyl-release-of-music-by-ruth-anderson/)]
