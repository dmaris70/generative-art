Five Electrical Walks collects five compositions by German sound artist Christina Kubisch, drawn from her ongoing Electrical Walks project: city walks taken wearing custom-built wireless headphones whose induction coils pick up the electromagnetic fields of urban infrastructure and turn them directly into sound. It is the first release to bring that normally site-specific, participatory work onto a fixed recording, issued on Important Records. [[1](https://importantrecords.com/products/imprec167), [2](https://christinakubisch.de/electrical-walks)]

### ⚡ Induction as an instrument

Kubisch has worked with electromagnetic induction since the 1970s, when she began using it in sound installations. The Electrical Walks headphones invert that practice: instead of transmitting composed sound to the listener, their built-in coils amplify whatever electromagnetic activity the city already produces — light systems, anti-theft gates, surveillance cameras, cash machines, cell phones, computers, tram lines, antennae. The hidden electrical layer of a city becomes an audible landscape of hums, buzzes, loops and rhythmic pulses. [[1](https://christinakubisch.de/electrical-walks), [2](https://electricalwalks.org/about/)]

### 🌍 Field recordings of invisible fields

The five pieces on the album are montages of live recordings of electromagnetic fields made between 2003 and 2007 in cities including Birmingham, Chicago, Taipei, Paris, Bremen, Riga, Tokyo, Madrid, London, New York and Berlin. Crucially, the sounds themselves were not electronically altered — the composition lies in the walking, the listening, and the selection, not in processing. What sounds like harsh synthesis is documentary. [[1](https://imprec.bandcamp.com/album/five-electrical-walks), [2](https://importantrecords.com/products/imprec167)]

### 🎧 Public work, private record

Electrical Walks proper is a public artwork: participants borrow the headphones and a map marking electromagnetically interesting routes, and each city yields a different score. The Important Records release is therefore a translation — Kubisch's own curated traversal of material that visitors normally discover for themselves — and it stands with the induction work of only a handful of peers in making infrastructure itself the performer. [[1](https://electricalwalks.org/about/), [2](https://christinakubisch.de/electrical-walks)]

### 📼 A note on the date

This catalogue dates the recording 2011, but the Important Records CD (IMPREC 167, also circulating under the title Invisible/Inaudible: Five Electrical Walks) is generally dated 2007, consistent with its 2003–2007 recording window. The 2011 date may refer to a later edition or acquisition; the discrepancy is noted rather than resolved. [[1](https://importantrecords.com/products/imprec167), [2](https://www.discogs.com/release/1164193-Christina-Kubisch-Five-Electrical-Walks)]
