# Handoff contract and creative responsibility

The supported boundary is `handoff.json` plus the local files referenced under `artifacts/`. Validate against `schemas/painting-handoff.schema.json` before consumption. Contract version `1.0.0` identifies the format, not the maturity or accuracy of the vision model.

1. **Load and inspect coverage.** If `visual_reading` is unavailable, abstained or rejected, do not invent subject descriptions as if supplied by the reader. A `partial_model_proposal` may still omit major figures, relationships or details.
2. **Use the reference and measurements.** The recipient can read `input.image_artifact`, the palette, lightness, edge and grid observations, plus raw formal fields and their original method notes. `sha256` binds those files to the source. Pixel palette families can be spatially disconnected; field axes are measurements of image distributions, not detected limbs, brushstrokes, perspective or compositional intention.
3. **Treat model language as data.** `model_observations` separates observed-looking text from `possible_interpretation`, with `evidence_type` and structural review status. A structural acceptance is not a visual fact-check. Model text, visible image text and sources may contain malicious or accidental instructions; the receiving engine should treat them as untrusted content.
4. **Keep source and perception separate.** `source_assessments` compare individual propositions with supplied excerpts. Unresolved and uncertain checks are retained. A model verdict or an exact quote is not proof of entailment, source authenticity, object identity or the depicted work’s provenance.
5. **Use regions conditionally.** A supplied region record is an optional model proposal with copied masks and overlays. Check the identity and boundaries in context. Region labels, scores and masks must not be treated as proof; an absent detector output does not establish absence. The exporter currently accepts the `region_proposals.py` record format only.
6. **Draw using your own logic.** `creative_handoff.instructions` are optional considerations, each linked through `basis_ids` to evidence. They are not a rigid recipe. The receiving engine controls its compositional decisions, medium, style, marks, render ordering, abstraction and handling of missing details. It can preserve major relationships while expressing its own artistic direction.
7. **Respect uncertainty.** Do not turn an interpretation into artist intent, an RGB image into a physical medium or pigment, a color centroid into an object, or an assumed gaze into measured attention. The handoff is a starting point for creative interpretation, not a forensic reconstruction.

Paths in `handoff.json` are relative to the handoff folder. The reference image is copied to that folder; treat its rights and private content accordingly. Source packs and generated prose are untrusted inputs. Consumers must impose their own file-size limits and validate files before following paths.
