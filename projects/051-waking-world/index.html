Each spark is one edit to a Wikipedia article, placed where that language's readers live; warm sparks come from daylight, blue from the dark. Titles are the articles being written, in their own script, with Wikipedia's own English title beneath; amber titles are new articles. Below, each language's 24-hour rhythm: by UTC the curves are staggered; by local sun time they fall onto one human day.
