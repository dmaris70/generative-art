The logbook of the journey

One sheet per week of the homage: both crossings between the Centre for Dance Studies in Athens and the Royal Ballet School in London, the eight milestone terms each crossing carried, where each term was first danced, and the lists. Drawn in single strokes, A3, ready for a pen plotter. The same clock decides the sheet and the wall, so the sheet records what the meadow showed.
